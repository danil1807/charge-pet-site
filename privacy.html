Charge Pet Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

Charge Pet does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any personal data. The app has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party SDKs, and it does not connect to the internet.

What stays on your iPhone

The app keeps a local log of game events, such as charging sessions, battery percentage at those moments and when you pet your pet, together with your settings. This data is used only to calculate your pet's state. It stays in the app's storage on your device and may be included in your own device backups.

Shortcuts automations

If you create automations in Apple's Shortcuts app, they tell Charge Pet that an event happened, for example that a charger was connected. Charge Pet cannot read your other shortcuts or see which other apps you use.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove all of its data from your iPhone.

Children

Charge Pet does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
