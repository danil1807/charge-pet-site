Charge Pet Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

Charge Pet does not sell or share personal data and has no account, no advertising and no tracking. Two things leave your iPhone: requests for pet artwork, and records of in-app purchases, both described below.

Downloaded pet artwork

To get new pet artwork and fixed Shortcuts files without an app update, the app downloads them from the developer's page on GitHub Pages (danil1807.github.io). The request contains no account, no identifiers and nothing from your game log. Like any website, GitHub receives your IP address and standard technical details of the request (app version, system language) to deliver the files, and the files requested depend on which pets you have.

In-app purchases

Purchases are made through Apple's App Store; Charge Pet never sees your payment details. The app turns on RevenueCat, a service that checks purchases and shows us sales statistics, only when you buy, restore a purchase, or the App Store reports a renewal, refund or approval of a purchase. From then on the app sends RevenueCat the purchase record (which product, when, and Apple's transaction ID), a random ID that RevenueCat generates for the app, and basic device information: device model, iOS and app version, language, App Store country and an identifier Apple gives the app on this device. Like any network request, it also reveals your IP address. None of this is tied to your name, email or Apple ID. Apple separately tells RevenueCat about renewals and refunds of the subscription. See RevenueCat's privacy policy. If you never buy or restore anything, nothing is sent to RevenueCat.

What stays on your iPhone

The app keeps a local log of game events, such as charging sessions, battery percentage at those moments and when you pet your pet, together with your settings. This data is used only to calculate your pet's state. It stays in the app's storage on your device and may be included in your own device backups.

Shortcuts automations

If you create automations in Apple's Shortcuts app, they tell Charge Pet that an event happened, for example that a charger was connected. If you set up the optional social app automations, the local log also keeps the time they ran, but not which app was opened. Charge Pet cannot read your other shortcuts or see which other apps you use.

Deleting your data

Delete the app to remove all of its data from your iPhone. To delete purchase records held by RevenueCat, write to the address below.

Children

Charge Pet does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Purchase records are anonymous, and purchases by children go through Apple's Ask to Buy.
