Questions or a bug to report? Write to danil.klimentev@gmail.com.

My pet doesn't react when I plug in

Open Charge Pet, open the Settings tab, tap Automations and check that the three automations exist in the Shortcuts app with "Run Immediately" turned on.

I don't see the Dynamic Island

The Dynamic Island needs an iPhone model that has it. Other iPhones show your pet on the Lock Screen and in widgets.
